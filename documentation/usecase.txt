Use Case Analysis
=====================================================

## Explanation
The Use Case diagram illustrates the primary interactions between the actors and the system. The system accommodates two main actors: the Public User (visitor) and the Administrator. 
- Public Users can browse the organizational history, view structural information, read articles, view documentation, and initiate SK (Surat Keputusan) submissions.
- Administrators have elevated privileges. They must authenticate (login) to access the dashboard. Once authenticated, they can manage (create, update, delete) articles and documentations, as well as handle media uploads.

## Related Files
- `app/page.tsx`
- `app/admin/layout.tsx`
- `app/api/login/route.ts`
- `app/api/articles/route.ts`

## Inferred Actors/Entities/Components
- Actors: Public User, Administrator
- Use Cases: Browse Content, Read Articles, Submit SK, Login, Manage Articles, Manage Documentations, Upload Media.

## Relationships
- The Administrator actor inherits or encompasses some basic viewing capabilities but primarily interacts with the management use cases.
- "Manage Articles" and "Manage Documentations" `<include>` the "Upload Media" use case.
