Entity Relationship Diagram (ERD) & Database Inference
=====================================================

## Explanation
The ERD reflects the relational database schema defined in `prisma/schema.prisma`. 
- **Admin**: Stores credentials for dashboard access.
- **Article**: Stores individual blog/news posts. No explicit foreign key connects to Admin, instead relying on a simple string `authorName`.
- **Documentation**: Stores events or gallery collections.
- **DocumentationImage**: Stores individual image URLs linked to a Documentation entry.

The structure implies standard CRUD operations on Articles and Documentations, with cascading deletes applied to DocumentationImage when its parent Documentation is removed.

## Related Files
- `prisma/schema.prisma`
- `prisma/migrations/*`

## Inferred Actors/Entities/Components
- Entities: `Admin`, `Article`, `Documentation`, `DocumentationImage`.

## Relationships
- `Documentation` (1) to `DocumentationImage` (N).
- Cascade delete constraint on `DocumentationImage.documentationId` referencing `Documentation.id`.
